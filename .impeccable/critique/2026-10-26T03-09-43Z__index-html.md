## Priority Issues
- **[P1] Video Codec Compatibility**:
  - *Why it matters*: Video source uses `Project MOON.mov` (`video/mp4` mime override). QuickTime `.mov` containers fail to play natively on many Android browsers and desktop Chrome configurations without H.264/AAC MP4 conversion.
  - *Fix*: Transcode video to standard `mp4` (H.264 + AAC) or webm, and provide multiple `<source>` tags.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable harden`
- **[P2] Mobile Navigation Header Spacing**:
  - *Why it matters*: On small mobile viewports (`<600px`), the sticky header wraps into multiple lines and takes up excessive vertical viewport height, pushing hero title content off-screen.
  - *Fix*: Reduce header padding, collapse nav links into a compact bar or inline horizontal scroll on mobile.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable adapt`
- **[P2] Focus Ring Contrast & Touch Targets**:
  - *Why it matters*: Dashed focus indicator (`#c9c4ba`) on dark background `#241e17` drops below 4.5:1 contrast in certain section contexts; modal close button target size is small on touch devices.
  - *Fix*: Increase focus outline contrast and ensure all touch targets meet minimum 44x44px.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable polish`
