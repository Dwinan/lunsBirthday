# Surface Brief: Moon's Birthday (Main Page)

## Scope and visitor mode
- Surface: Single page interactive birthday gift experience.
- Mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport; emotional resonance above all).

## Audience, job, action/task, proof/content, and constraints
- Visitor: Moon (turning 20).
- Job: Feel deeply loved, nostalgic, and cherished through shared memories and a personal video.
- Content: Letter copy (modal), 4 polaroid memories with captions, Project MOON.mov video gift.
- Constraints: Static HTML/CSS/JS, no dynamic backend, video local file, texture background.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A film contact-sheet journal where memories are organized with structural warmth—eliminating random rotations and competing tapes for a deliberate, physical, tactile layout.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm paper ground (#fdf8f0 / #f2ebe0), deep ink (#241e17 / #362f27), warm accent wash (#e5989b), soft green tape accents (#8db596), strict alignment with subtle hand-drawn touches (Caveat for headings, Patrick Hand for body), film negative borders, and crisp paper edges.

### STORY
The visitor moves from a quiet opening frame into a personal written letter, through a curated film contact-sheet of four core memories, landing on a ceremonial video screening box that unwraps like a gift.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A clean floating journal header; hero viewport features a contact-frame card holding a warm moon emblem, a clean handwritten greeting, a single subtle rose tape, and an invitation to scroll.

### FORM
Contact sheet + handwritten notes journal.
